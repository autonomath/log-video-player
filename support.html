Getting started

  1. Open Log Video Player once from your Applications folder.
  2. Drag a Log video onto the app, choose File → Open, or—after the default-viewer setup below—double-click it.
  3. Check the badge above the controls to see the camera profile and Rec.709 transform being used.

The app reads your selected video for playback only. It never requires Full Disk Access and never modifies the original file.

Make it your default player

Choose Make default viewer… on the welcome screen or in Settings. macOS requires this one-time change in Finder:

  1. Choose an MP4 or MOV video. Log Video Player reveals it in Finder.
  2. Press ⌘I to open Get Info.
  3. Under Open with, choose Log Video Player, click Change All…, then confirm.

The setting applies to that file type. Repeat the same steps once for MOV if you started with MP4, or for MP4 if you started with MOV.

Picture adjustments

Click the slider icon in the playback controls to adjust Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, Temperature and Tint while the video plays.

  • Adjustments are applied after the active Log conversion and affect the viewer only.
  • Use the arrow beside a control to reset it, or choose Reset All to return all five controls to neutral.
  • Switching to Original bypasses both the Log conversion and picture adjustments. Returning to Normal color restores your current values.

Picture adjustments never edit, render or export the video. They are unavailable during native HDR playback and while Original is active.

Troubleshooting

The video still looks flat

Open the profile menu and confirm that a Log profile is active. If the badge says Assumed or Rec.709 passthrough, choose the profile used in your camera. The app remembers that camera signature for next time.

A camera profile is unavailable

Supported DJI profiles show Get Official LUT and open the matching DJI download page. Apple Log 2, Canon Log, GoPro Log and custom profiles show Profile information. Import the compatible .cube once; installed profiles stay local and work offline.

The video will not open

Confirm the copy has finished and that the file is MP4, MOV or M4V using H.264, HEVC or ProRes. Try opening it directly from inside the app. A damaged or incomplete camera file may not be playable.

Playback is not smooth

Close other graphics-heavy apps and try the file from faster local storage. Log Video Player uses the hardware-accelerated playback path available on your Mac. Report the Mac model, macOS version, codec and frame rate if the issue continues.

The wrong profile was selected

Search or browse the profile menu above the playback controls—even while the video is playing. Automatic detection intentionally yields to your manual choice, and the correction is saved by camera signature—not by private filename.

Picture controls are unavailable

Return from Original to Normal color. Controls also remain unavailable for native HDR footage so macOS can preserve its HDR display path.

System requirements

  • Apple silicon Mac
  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later
  • MP4, MOV or M4V video
  • H.264, HEVC—including 10-bit—or ProRes video codec

Performance depends on video resolution, frame rate, codec and storage speed. HDR footage is displayed using the Mac and display capabilities available to macOS.

Request help

Official support contact

Log Video Player support is provided by developer Mathieu Gagnon through the public GitHub Issues channel linked below. Search existing reports or open a new support request there.

Include the app version, macOS version, Mac model, camera model, codec, resolution and frame rate.

Do not upload private footage. A screenshot of the profile badge and non-sensitive metadata is usually enough to begin.
