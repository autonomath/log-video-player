Privacy policy

Your footage is yours.

Log Video Player is designed to watch local video—not to collect information about you or your work.

On this page

Plain-language summary Data in the app Optional downloads This website Support Policy changes

Plain-language summary

Log Video Player does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. Your videos are processed locally on your Mac and are never uploaded by the app.

There are no user accounts, advertisements, analytics trackers, behavioural profiles or cloud-processing services in Log Video Player.

The app does not modify your original videos and does not create converted copies. It reads only the files you choose to open.

Data in the app

Video files

Playback, metadata inspection, colour transforms and live picture adjustments happen on your device. Your footage, filenames, camera metadata and viewing activity are not sent to us.

Settings

The app stores camera-profile overrides and installed profile data locally. These settings stay on your Mac and can be removed by deleting the app’s stored data.

Apple services

Apple may process App Store purchases, receipts, downloads and diagnostics according to your Apple and macOS settings. Log Video Player does not use that information to track you. Review Apple’s Privacy Policy for details.

Optional camera-profile downloads

Some camera transforms require a LUT from an official camera-manufacturer website. If you choose Get Official LUT, the app opens that manufacturer’s download page in your default browser. The website may receive technical information such as your IP address and request time under its own privacy policy.

You choose the downloaded .cube file to install. Supported official LUTs are verified before use, stored locally and work offline afterward. The app does not send video content or camera metadata to the manufacturer.

This website

This site uses no cookies, advertising or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process limited connection data needed to deliver the pages and protect its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Support requests

Support is provided through public GitHub Issues. Anything you choose to post there is handled by GitHub and may be publicly visible. Please do not attach private footage, private file paths or other sensitive information.

We use support information only to answer your request and improve the app. You can edit or remove your GitHub post using the controls provided by GitHub.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date. Material changes will be explained plainly.

Questions about privacy can be submitted to developer Mathieu Gagnon through the public GitHub Issues support channel.
