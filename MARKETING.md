### Feature proof points

- The custom timeline tracks the pointer continuously and shows buffered,
  elapsed and remaining positions.
- Hover previews pass through the active camera transform, LUT, Original /
  Normal Colour mode and picture adjustments.
- Playback speeds: 0.25×, 0.5×, 1×, 1.5×, 2× and 4×.
- Keyboard transport: J/K/L shuttle, comma/period frame step, left/right
  10-second jumps and Space play/pause.
- Preview frames are held in memory only. No thumbnail cache, sidecar,
  converted copy or original-file write is created.

## Version 1.1 App Store description

Flat Log footage should not require an editing project just to watch it.

Log Video Player detects supported camera Log profiles and displays them through a real-time Rec.709 viewing transform. After a one-time Finder setup, double-click a file—or drag it onto the app or choose Open. Your original video is never changed.

AUTOMATIC NORMAL COLOUR
• Built-in ACES-based transforms for DJI D-Log, Apple Log, Sony S-Log2 and S-Log3, Canon Log 2 and 3, and Panasonic V-Log
• Camera-specific DJI D-Log and D-Log M profiles with verified one-time official LUT import where required
• Search and switch camera profiles while playback continues
• Clearly identifies the active conversion and leaves ordinary SDR and HDR footage untouched

LIVE PICTURE ADJUSTMENTS
• Tune brightness, contrast, saturation, temperature and tint during playback
• Adjustments are applied after Log conversion and never affect the original
• Reset any setting—or all five—instantly

SIMPLE, NON-DESTRUCTIVE PLAYBACK
• No projects, timelines, exports or duplicate files
• Hardware-accelerated 10-bit HEVC playback
• Smooth colour-corrected scrubbing with hover previews and exact time
• Playback speeds, frame stepping, 10-second jumps and J/K/L shuttle controls
• Instant Original / Normal colour comparison
• Manual profiles and custom .cube LUTs when needed

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Footage remains on your Mac
• No account, advertising, analytics or cloud uploads
• Works offline after required camera profiles are installed

Log Video Player is an independent product and is not affiliated with any camera manufacturer. Product names are used only to describe compatibility.
